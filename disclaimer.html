Education disclaimer

Understand the scope of this website.

General education

Capital & Freedom provides general financial education and shares Namita’s personal experience. Its articles, emails, tools, and social content are not personalized investment, tax, or legal advice. The site does not provide portfolio management or execute transactions for you.

Your circumstances matter

No self-check or worksheet determines which product is suitable for you. Personal decisions depend on your circumstances, obligations, goals, and the terms of a product. Investment values can fall and capital can be lost. Namita’s experience does not predict your outcome.

No product recommendations

The site does not recommend specific securities or promise returns. Examples are for understanding concepts. Verify current information with the official provider and consult an appropriately registered professional when you need personal investment advice.

Learning at your pace

Completing a learning path does not require you to invest. Take time to understand a decision before acting. For questions about this material, contact mail@capitalandfreedom.com.
