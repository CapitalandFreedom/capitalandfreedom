A few things you might wonder

No question is too basic.

A little clarity before you begin.

Do I need to know anything about investing?

No. Begin with your household money and account access. The lessons introduce unfamiliar words as they come up.

Can I use the tools without giving my email?

Yes. The self-check and printable Money Map are open to everyone. Subscribing is optional.

Will you see my test answers or financial details?

The self-check runs in your browser. We do not send or save your answers or score. The Money Map is a blank worksheet for you to complete privately.

Is this available in Hindi?

The self-check is available in English and Roman-script Hinglish. You can choose either language preference for the newsletter. The main website is currently in English.

Are you offering a course or paid advice?

The launch is focused on free education and the newsletter. No paid course, personal investment advice, or portfolio management service is offered here.

Can I learn while my husband continues to help?

Of course. Understanding and participating can sit alongside support from your family. You can decide how to share responsibilities.

Will financial independence mean leaving my job?

It does not have to. Namita chose to retire. Your goal may be more security, more choices, or confidence in decisions while continuing to work.
