Why I started Capital & Freedom

I’m Namita. I’m 40, a mother, and financially independent. I spent 15 years working in corporate. I invested sensibly over time, and reaching financial independence gave me the option to retire after motherhood and spend more time with my family.

That choice mattered to me.

I know there are women who earn a salary every month but leave investing and financial decisions to their husbands. Often, it is simply how things have always worked. Nobody taught them, someone else was willing to handle it, and life was busy. Then years pass, and asking a basic question can start to feel more uncomfortable than continuing with the arrangement.

A place to ask the basic questions

Capital & Freedom is for the woman who wants to start understanding. You might need to find your statements before learning about investments. You might want help starting a conversation at home. Or you might be ready to make sense of the products you already own.

I’ll explain things in English and Hinglish, with the patience I would want from an older sister. We’ll begin with your everyday money and build from there.

Your freedom can look different from mine

You may love your work and want to keep doing it. You may want a career break, more security for your family, or simply the confidence to understand a document before signing it. Retirement is my story. Having a say in your money can be yours.
