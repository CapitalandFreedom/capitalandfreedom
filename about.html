More women.
More say in their lives.

A world where every woman has the financial confidence to make life’s biggest decisions on her own terms.

Why this matters to me

I’m Namita. I’m 40, a mother, and financially independent. I worked in corporate for 15 years and invested sensibly over time. After motherhood, I chose to retire so I could spend more time with my family and live with more freedom.

Money made that choice possible.

Your choice may look different. You might want to keep working with less anxiety about the future, take time out to care for someone, buy a home, or simply understand what you are agreeing to. I want more women to feel able to ask the questions, understand the answers, and take part in the financial decisions connected to the lives they want.

What I’m here to help with

I help women who feel intimidated by money build financial confidence through simple education, practical investing knowledge, and supportive conversations, so they can make life’s biggest decisions on their own terms.

We’ll learn through everyday situations and useful exercises. The scorecard helps you notice your starting point. The Money Reset gives you room to reflect. The Money Date helps turn an intention into a regular habit.

What you can expect from me

Honesty
I will never promise unrealistic returns or easy wealth.
Simplicity
I explain money in language that does not make women feel intimidated.
Evidence over hype
I explain sound principles and their limits, rather than chase trends or use fear to rush a decision.
Empathy
Every woman starts somewhere. There is no shame in starting late.
Independence
My goal is to help you make informed decisions yourself, without needing to depend on me forever.

A supportive place to begin

For now, you can learn through the free tools and Sunday Letter, reply with a question, or join the conversation on Instagram and YouTube. There is no paid membership or separate community platform to join.
