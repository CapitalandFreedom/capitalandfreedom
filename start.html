Your first month

You don’t need to learn it all today.

Begin with what you already have. These five steps are a suggested learning path, at your own pace.

01

See the full picture

List what comes in, what goes out, what you own, and what you owe.

02

Get comfortable with access

Learn how to find statements and use your own accounts securely.

03

Build your savings habit

Understand your essential expenses and plan money for unexpected needs.

04

Learn the investing basics

Make sense of risk, time, costs, and the words people use.

05

Practice making a decision

Learn which questions to ask before choosing an investment.

One month is a starting point, not a deadline. You do not need to buy an investment to complete this path.
